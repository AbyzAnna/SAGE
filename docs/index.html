A calendar that listens, not one you click.
SAGE is a local, private scheduling assistant. You write a sentence — "study calc tomorrow 2–4, dentist Thursday 3pm" — and a real, structured week assembles itself.
No cloud. No API keys. No subscription. The model lives on your machine, so your week stays yours.

Construction

Drawn from a single seed point, the leaf grows downward — wider as it descends, like a week filling in.

The mark is built on a 6-unit grid. Two arcs meet at the tip (apex) and base (root). The vertical vein is exactly 1.6px at production scale and divides the form into perfect symmetry.

04 / Voice
In practice.
SAGE's voice is the calm friend who already understands what you mean — and gently shows you the receipts.
Principles

Speak in full sentences. Answer in structured calm.

Users describe their lives the way they think — half-formed, run-on, abbreviated. SAGE never asks them to slow down or reformat. It listens, then quietly produces a real schedule, with the parsing shown as a receipt the user can verify.

Tone: warm, brief, never cute. Confirms what it did. Surfaces what it didn't. Apologises once, fixes it, moves on.

Never · "I'd be happy to" · "Certainly!" · emoji · exclamation marks

01
Echo, don't parrot.
Confirm the action in your own words — not a verbatim repeat of the user's input.
02
Show the receipt.
Every change comes with a structured summary the user can scan and verify in under two seconds.
03
Offer the next move.
When you catch an adjacent intent, ask once — never assume, never nag.

06 / The promise
Your week is yours.
Your data is yours.
Your calendar should listen.

SAGE is not a SaaS. It is a small piece of software that lives on your machine and writes down what you tell it.

There is no account. There is no cloud. There is no growth target, no upsell, no engagement loop.

There is a model, a calendar, and you — describing your week out loud until the week exists.
